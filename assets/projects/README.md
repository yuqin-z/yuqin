## How to add images, videos, or external links
1. **Images / local videos**: Drop your image(s) (e.g. `cover.jpg`, `01.png`, `diagram.svg`) or video (`demo.mp4`) into the corresponding project folder, or paste external video/website URLs in its `links.md`.
2. **Displaying on the page**: Each `<article class="portfolio-entry">` in `research.html`, `design.html`, and `tools.html` has a `data-project-folder="assets/projects/..."` attribute and a ready-to-use `.project-links` / `.image-placeholder` block where images, videos, or website links can be enabled.
