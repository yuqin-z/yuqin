Summary

I am a PhD researcher at the intersection of spatial cognition, built environment and human-centred design. My research investigates how people perceive, navigate, and experience built environments through behavioural experiments in virtual environments, multimodal data collection, and computational spatial analysis.

I also develop research tools (experimental platforms and simulation) to understand human experience and collaborate with design practices. My longer-term goal is to develop and validate predictive methods to support pre-occupancy human-centred evaluation of spaces.

Research & professional experience

PhD Researcher

2023–present

Architectural Cognition in Practice, Future Cities Lab Global, Singapore-ETH Centre, Singapore

Design Intention vs User Experience in Complex Buildings

  • Designed and developed Move360, a configurable web-based 360° behavioural experimentation platform (React/TypeScript/Vite frontend, Supabase backend). It supports experiment configuration, interactive navigation, multimodal data collection (trajectories, interaction logs, gaze, verbal reports), and visualisation.
  • With the developed platform, conducted a desktop-based wayfinding study with 70 participants, collecting human behaviour, Tobii eye-tracking, Shimmer GSR, think-aloud data, and subjective experience ratings.
  • Developed route-sequence and spatial attention analysis; exploring computer vision and AI approaches for relating visual environmental features to human attention and navigation decisions.

Computational Spatial Analysis for Human-Centred Design

  • Developed a taxonomy of spatial analysis methods and tools to support evidence-informed method selection.
  • Prototyped a Python-based LLM research agent (OpenAI Agents SDK, structured extraction, verification) to synthesise 105 papers into 177 spatial metric–human outcome relationships.

Translating Architectural Cognition into Design Practice

  • Collaborate with industry partners (e.g. UNS Amsterdam; WOHA Singapore) on pre/post-occupancy projects worldwide.
  • Developed an operational five-principle framework for integrating cognitive/behavioural research into practice.
  • Applied cognitive mapping, computational spatial analysis, agent-based modelling, and VR-based user testing to evaluate design alternatives and provide data-based feedback to designers.
